01. About Me

Hi! I'm Gerardo. I've been shipping software for 20+ years — riding the wave from early big-data on Hadoop and Spark, through the deep learning revolution (CNNs, RNNs, computer vision on edge), straight into today's frontier of multimodal LLM systems, agentic architectures and real-time voice AI on NVIDIA H100.

Today I lead AI/ML at Pragmatic / Neuranta, where we ship a real-time conversational AI agent that fuses Whisper ASR, neural TTS and tool-calling LLMs over a hybrid RAG layer backed by Milvus — orchestrated on Kubernetes and served on H100 GPUs with sub-second time-to-first-token. Before that: AI-driven cybersecurity, real-time POS analytics across Alsea brands (Starbucks, Domino's), and large-scale SCADA telemetry over 1,500 industrial gas wells.

The thread across two decades stays the same: ship AI that runs in production at scale — not in slides.
